About This Property

Set dramatically on the cliffs of the Amalfi Coast, Azure Cliff Villa is a masterpiece of modern Mediterranean architecture. Designed by award-winning architect Luca Ferretti, the villa harmonizes organic stone and glass to create a home that feels as though it emerged from the rock itself.

The property offers sweeping 180-degree views of the Tyrrhenian Sea from every principal room. Six generously proportioned en-suite bedrooms open to private terraces, while the infinity pool appears to dissolve into the horizon at the cliff's edge.

A fully equipped chef's kitchen, home cinema, wine cellar, and two-level terrace with al fresco dining complete this extraordinary offering. Available furnished to the current owner's meticulous standard.
