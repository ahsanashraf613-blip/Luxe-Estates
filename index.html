✦ Our Story

Redefining Luxury
Real Estate

Founded in 1998 by Sir Edmund Ashworth, Luxe Estates began with a single conviction: that acquiring exceptional property should be as extraordinary as the property itself. From a boutique London office, we've grown into a globally recognized authority in luxury real estate.

Today, our 200+ specialists operate across 26 markets, advising some of the world's most discerning buyers and sellers. We don't just sell homes — we match people with places that reflect who they are.

Our philosophy is rooted in discretion, deep market expertise, and an unwavering commitment to client satisfaction. We represent only the finest 1% of properties globally.
